      Where this number comes from

      No fine print — here is exactly how the tool works.

      Colombia's official rate

      We use the TRM (Tasa Representativa del Mercado), the official exchange rate published by Colombia's Financial Superintendency. It is the country's daily benchmark and the figure banks and companies use for invoicing.

      Checked on every visit

      Every time you open this page the source is queried and the most recent published value is shown, along with the date it took effect. The TRM changes once per business day, so that is the freshest figure available.

      A reference, not a settlement

      The TRM is the official benchmark for invoicing and for the day's accounting. A real transaction at a bank or exchange office settles at that institution's own rate plus its fees, so it can close at a different value.

      Always available

      If the official source is unreachable at that moment, the tool falls back to a market rate and tells you on screen. You will never see a figure without knowing where it came from.
